Who We Are

Crestpoint was founded to bridge the gap between design ambition and construction reality. We provide end-to-end consultancy services — from feasibility studies and design management to on-site supervision and project delivery. Our mission is to deliver safe, sustainable, and high-quality projects on time and within budget. With extensive experience across Uganda’s public and private sectors, Crestpoint stands out for its precision, transparency, and safety-first culture.
